# AI Risk Assessment

## AI Governance Domains

- Model governance
- AI explainability
- Prompt injection protection
- Data leakage prevention
- AI output validation
- Human oversight
- AI model monitoring
- AI bias assessment
- AI vendor transparency
- AI sub-processor governance

## AI Risk Categories

| Risk | Description |
|---|---|
| Prompt Injection | Manipulation of model behavior |
| Data Leakage | Exposure of sensitive information |
| Hallucination | Inaccurate AI-generated output |
| Model Poisoning | Compromise of training data |
| Bias | Unfair or discriminatory outcomes |
| Shadow AI | Unapproved AI usage |

## Required Controls

- AI governance policy
- AI usage restrictions
- Human approval workflows
- AI activity logging
- AI risk assessments
- AI transparency controls
