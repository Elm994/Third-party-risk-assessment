# Third-Party Security Questionnaire

## Governance

1. Do you maintain an information security program?
2. Is your security program aligned to ISO 27001 or NIST?
3. Is executive leadership involved in cybersecurity governance?
4. Do you maintain cybersecurity policies?
5. Are policies reviewed annually?

## Identity & Access Management

6. Is MFA enforced for privileged users?
7. Is MFA enforced for remote access?
8. Are privileged accounts monitored?
9. Are dormant accounts disabled?
10. Are password policies enforced?

## Logging & Monitoring

11. Are security logs centrally collected?
12. Are logs protected from tampering?
13. Is SIEM technology implemented?
14. Are alert thresholds documented?
15. Is suspicious activity monitored 24/7?

## Vulnerability Management

16. Are vulnerability scans conducted regularly?
17. Are critical vulnerabilities remediated within SLA?
18. Is penetration testing performed annually?
19. Are external assets continuously monitored?
20. Is patch management centrally managed?

## Incident Response

21. Is there a documented incident response plan?
22. Are tabletop exercises conducted?
23. Are customers notified during incidents?
24. Are forensic procedures documented?
25. Are incidents classified by severity?

## Business Continuity & Resilience

26. Is there a business continuity plan?
27. Is disaster recovery tested annually?
28. Are backups encrypted?
29. Are backups immutable?
30. Are recovery objectives documented?

## Cloud & Infrastructure Security

31. Are cloud environments hardened?
32. Is infrastructure-as-code reviewed?
33. Are production environments segregated?
34. Are secrets securely managed?
35. Is EDR deployed across endpoints?

## Data Protection & Privacy

36. Is data encrypted at rest?
37. Is data encrypted in transit?
38. Are data retention periods defined?
39. Is customer data segregated?
40. Are international data transfers disclosed?

## Supplier & Supply Chain Security

41. Do you assess your own suppliers?
42. Are sub-processors disclosed?
43. Are software dependencies monitored?
44. Is SBOM generation supported?
45. Are third-party integrations reviewed?

## AI Governance & Security

46. Do AI systems process customer data?
47. Are prompts logged?
48. Is AI training data isolated?
49. Are AI outputs reviewed?
50. Is adversarial testing performed?
