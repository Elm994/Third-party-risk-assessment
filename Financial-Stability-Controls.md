# Financial Stability Controls

## Assessment Areas

- Revenue trends
- Profitability
- Debt obligations
- Bankruptcy indicators
- Funding stability
- Customer concentration risk
- Cyber insurance coverage
- Operational sustainability

## Required Evidence

- Audited financial statements
- Cyber insurance certificates
- Credit reports
- Regulatory disclosures
- Public financial reports

## High-Risk Indicators

- Significant layoffs
- Negative cash flow
- Declining customer base
- Pending litigation
- Missed regulatory filings
