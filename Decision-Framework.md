# Decision Framework

| Outcome | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Approved | Low residual risk |
| Approved with Conditions | Moderate findings with remediation plan |
| Deferred | Significant remediation required |
| Rejected | Unacceptable risk exposure |

## Mandatory Rejection Triggers

- No MFA
- No incident response capability
- Unencrypted sensitive data
- Undisclosed sub-processors
- Severe financial instability
- Unsafe AI processing practices
