# Third-party-risk-assessment Framework
ISO 27001:2022 | APRA CPS 231/234 | NIST 800-53 | SOC 2 | NIST AI RMF

An enterprise-grade Third-Party Risk Assessment (TPRA) framework designed for assessing vendors, suppliers, SaaS platforms, AI providers, cloud environments, managed service providers, and critical third-party relationships.

This repository demonstrates a modern, regulator-aligned, Big-4-style TPRA methodology covering:

- Vendor security assessments
- AI governance and AI risk management
- Financial stability analysis
- Continuous monitoring
- Threat-informed supplier assurance
- Enterprise risk scoring
- Data governance and classification
- Supply chain resilience

---

# Modern Threat Landscape

Recent global incidents including:

- SolarWinds
- MOVEit
- Okta
- Snowflake
- CrowdStrike

have demonstrated that third-party vendors represent one of the largest enterprise attack surfaces.

This framework was designed to address modern supply-chain threats using:

- Risk-based assessment methodologies
- Continuous monitoring concepts
- AI governance controls
- Security maturity scoring
- Regulatory alignment

# How to Use This Questionnaire

## Step 1 — Send the Questionnaire
Request:

- Policies
- Certifications
- Diagrams
- Evidence
- Audit reports

## Step 2 — Review Evidence
Validate:

- Control effectiveness
- Policy maturity
- Technical implementation
- Regulatory alignment

## Step 3 — Score Domains
Apply 0–5 scoring across all domains.

## Step 4 — Calculate Weighted Score
Use domain weighting methodology.

## Step 5 — Apply Risk Modifiers
Consider:

- AI exposure
- Privileged access
- Sensitive data handling
- Critical business dependency

## Step 6 — Document Findings
Record:

- Control gaps
- Risks
- Recommendations
- Residual risk

## Step 7 — Final Decision
Determine:

- Approve
- Approve with conditions
- Defer
- Reject
